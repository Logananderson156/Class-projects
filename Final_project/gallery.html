Exploring the World of Sword Art Online

Originally airing over a year ago in July 2012, Sword Art Online has become one of the most recognizable and controversial anime of all time. In reality, it's a fine series that's especially good at easing newcomers into the genre, with interesting game systems and visually intriguing worlds that hook its fans. Aside from the mechanics and design of the game worlds within Sword Art Online, one of the anime's main draws - especially compared to the source material, since animation helps breathe new life into these explosive moments - is its intense action scenes.

"Crimson Killing Intent" is Sword Art Online's tenth episode, and is well-remembered for Kirito's duel against Heathcliff, founder of the same guild Asuna's part of, Knights of the Blood Oath. Unbeknownst to Kirito, Heathcliff is actually the avatar of the Game Master, Akihiko Kayaba himself. Kirito duels him for the right to take Asuna on a vacation away from the guild.Although Kirito does land a scratch on him, due to Heathcliff holding back and hiding his true strength, Heathcliff easily wipes the floor with Kirito in front of a stadium of spectators. Most of Kirito's strategy here comes down to whacking Heathcliff's shield with his swords, but it's his anger and desperation that remain in fans' heads. The animation does a great job of showing just how outmatched Kirito is, despite giving his all, by his facial expressions. It's a quick several-minute, early-series fight that does well to set up the rest of the episode and Heathcliff's betrayal.

Just a few episodes before Season One's finale, "The Grand Quest" sees Kirito's first attempt at scaling the World Tree to reach and free Asuna. Steeling his might and summoning his wings, Kirito makes a desperately determined face before jetting up the World Tree as fast as he can. The Tree's Guardians come out of the walls and, though Kirito tries his best, he's quickly overpowered and defeated, sent slamming back to the bottom and having to respawn.

In Sword Art Online's ninth episode, "The Blue-Eyed Demon", Kirito and Asuna run from the 74th floor boss, before getting a group together to devise an attack strategy. An unrelated guild marches in and, realizing they're in grave danger, Kirito and Asuna follow them to the boss. This boss brings up memories of the Moonlit Black Cats incident for Kirito, as the room's enchanted, so no Teleport Crystals will work - just like the trap where all members of the guild aside from Kirito himself perished. After one guild member dies and several others are on the verge of death, Kirito shows off his new Dual Wielding skill to save everyone from The Gleam Eyes in an unforgettable battle. Kirito nearly dies and is just barely able to attack faster, landing the finishing blow. The angles and sword effects in this fight tie everything together, making this an impactful, well-animated fight that's hard to forget

Episode 31 of Season Three, "Brand of a Failure", showcases Alice's destructive power as she makes use of Sacred Arts to unleash Fire Burst Element. This manifests as a huge laser beam, a deadly explosion that's impossible to dodge and leaves nothing but death and destruction in its wake. The visuals as Alice charges and unleashes this skill are hauntingly beautiful, and she lingers afterward in contemplative silence as to the destruction she's caused. Alice raises her golden sword to the sky before commanding her flowers to burst, conjuring a giant golden flower in the sky that's fully rigged with Fire-type explosive beams. Its weight and destructive power can truly be felt as Alice unleashes her Burst Element, destroying everything in its wake. Alice is a kind and proud noble, so it's painful watching her commit such acts, with viewers' emotions only heightened by the scene's immaculate visuals.

"Absolute Sword", the nineteenth episode of Sword Art Online's second season and the second episode of the highly popular Mother's Rosario arc, heavily focuses on Asuna. After a particularly heated conversation with her mother, Asuna heads in-game to a duel she'd been challenged to by a mysterious player - a girl similar to her who also excels in swordplay, sharing Asuna's surname, Yuki. Both Asuna and Yuki's grit and determination are nicely on display during this battle, through both their facial expressions and the intense desperation of their sword swings. Near the end, it looks like Asuna's about to land a clean, final blow on Yuki, before being taken by surprise and finished off by a unique sword skill devised by Yuki herself.

Set between Season Two and Season Three of the Sword Art Online anime, Ordinal Scale is a canon part of the series and sees Kirito and his friends checking out a game of the same name. This movie's big threat is memory-scanning drones, which have the ability to scan and activate wide-scale brain shutdown, much like the original NervGear headset. In order to stop it, Kirito and his friends go up against the one-hundredth floor boss of Aincrad, named An Incarnation Of The Radius. Being a movie, this climactic boss fight from Ordinal Scale naturally has more impact and visual flair than most of the fights in Sword Art Online. It's also a memorable fight for including more characters than just Kirito and Asuna

The season finale fight of Alicization War of Underworld Part 2, Kirito's epic, climactic battle against Gabriel is every bit as explosive and well-animated as fans hoped. While some fans were disappointed this is yet another Kirito spotlight fight, other characters truly shine throughout Alicization, and it makes sense for a series like Sword Art Online to have Kirito pull off a beautiful dual wielding technique during a glorious one-on-one against the season's final boss. This battle, in addition to being beautiful, wraps up the storyline of Kirito and Eugeo's friendship nicely, which had been one of the focuses of Alicization. Kirito defeats Gabriel while shining with golden light, holding radiating pink and blue dual blades, and the climactic nature of the scene is further carried by a phenomenal soundtrack and top-tier animation.

Surprisingly, the widely agreed-upon best and most visually breathtaking scene in Sword Art Online doesn't involve either of the series' main protagonists. Instead, it's the ultimate fight between Bercouli and Lord Vecta that easily takes the cake for being the most impressively-animated fight. Even fans of the original Light Novel were blown away by how beautifully this fight was animated, with the art style surpassing even cinematic quality in some shots. Bercouli's rage and desperation to take down Lord Vecta is palpable, even at the cost of his own life. The fight starts with Bercouli on horseback, but he quickly dismounts to stand and face Lord Vecta on equal footing. He endures a hard-fought battle, taking tons of hits from Lord Vecta, waiting for the perfect moment to strike and finish things for good. He doesn't falter even when victory also results in his own death, and his sacrifice combine with unforgettably gorgeous visuals and a stellar OST to create the best scene in Sword Art Online's history.
