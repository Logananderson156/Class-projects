The beginning:Jurassic park

Jurassic park was created in 1993 directed by Steven Spielberg, and written by Micheal Crichton, and David koepp. Jurassic park started outh as a book, written by author crichton in 1990, and was turned into a movie.The movie is located on the island of Isla Nublar, close to Puerto Rico, were buissnessman Jhon Hammond, and his team of genetic scientists, created a park of dinosaurs. An industrial sabatoge shuts the parks power down, which give the dinaurs the chance escape their cages, causing choas all over as a grope of visitors sturggle to escape. THe dinosaurs in the movie were created computer generated imagery, and life-sized animatronics, using a mixture of animal sounds for the noises. The movie premired on June 9,1993 at uptown theater in Washington D.C,. and became a blockbuster hit grossing over $914 million in it original run.
